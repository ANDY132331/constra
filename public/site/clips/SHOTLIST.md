# Shot list — real clips of Constra in use

These go on the landing page. They have to be real people really using the app: your own
crew, or customers who have agreed on camera. Staged footage of actors presented as users is
a fabricated testimonial, and it is both misleading and against advertising rules.

## Before filming

- Ask everyone on camera, out loud, on the recording: "Can we use this on our website?"
- Shoot landscape, 1080p, 30 or 60 fps. Phone is fine — a real phone on a real site looks
  more honest than a studio.
- Film the screen and the person. A phone held in a gloved hand against a real backdrop is
  the whole point.
- Avoid capturing client names, addresses, licence plates, or anyone who did not agree.

## The six clips worth having

Each should stand alone in under 15 seconds, and read with no sound.

1. **Clock in at the gate** — worker arrives, opens the app, taps Clock In, takes the selfie,
   the "38 m from site · verified" badge appears. This is the product in one shot.
2. **Foreman checks who is on** — opens Time, scrolls the crew list, you see live hours
   counting. Shoot over the shoulder so the screen is readable.
3. **Photo from the deck** — someone photographs work in progress and it lands in the project.
   Shoot the real thing being photographed first, then the phone.
4. **Daily report in the truck** — end of day, cab of a pickup, report filled and sent.
   Golden hour through the windscreen sells this one.
5. **Invoice sent from the site** — owner on the tailgate, taps Send, the client email goes.
6. **Payroll on Friday** — laptop at the kitchen table or site office, hours exported.
   This is the "I got my evening back" shot.

## What makes them cinematic

- Shoot wide first, then one tight shot of the screen. Cutting between the two reads as
  real coverage rather than a screen recording.
- Hold each shot longer than feels natural — 4 to 6 seconds. Fast cuts look like an ad;
  slow ones look like evidence.
- Shoot early or late. Midday sun blows out both faces and phone screens.
- Keep the phone screen brightness at maximum or it will not read on camera.
- Let people work. The best take is usually the one after they forget the camera.

## After filming

1. Trim to the single beat that matters. One idea per clip.
2. Export an MP4 (H.264) and a poster frame as `.webp`.
3. Drop both in this folder and list them in `lib/site-clips.ts`.
