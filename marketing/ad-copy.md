# Constra ads: copy and a first test

Every claim below matches what the app does today. Don't add numbers you can't back up
("saves 10 hours a week") until real customers tell you so; ad platforms and customers both
punish claims that don't hold.

Crew Plan, Time Off and Email to payroll aren't in any ad yet: they switch on once the
database update is run. Add them after that.

## A first test that won't waste money

1. **Audience:** your country, ages 25–60, interests like construction, general contractor, renovation, electrician, plumbing, roofing, landscaping. Or start with your area only.
2. **Budget:** CAD $10–15 a day for 5–7 days.
3. **Creatives:** the 30s video plus three statics (paper, alert, texts). Let Meta spread the spend.
4. **Judge it on sign-ups**, not clicks or views. After a week, kill the worst two and put the money into the best one.
5. **Before spending a dollar:** run the database update, and test the sign-up and invite flow on a real phone. Ads that send people to something broken burn money.
