# Constra feature series

One short film per feature, so every video has one idea and nothing is crammed. The feature list comes from the app itself (the "Everything on the job" list on the home page plus the sections in the app). Status: done, in this batch, or queued.

Feature work that needs the database update (migrations 013, 014, 015) is marked HOLD. Make and post those videos only after the update is applied, or the feature fails for new users.
